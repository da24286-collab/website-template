Our History

The Maunga Club was founded in 1973 by a small group of passionate skiers led by Roslyn, inspired by the late Tweedly Dee. Their goal was to establish a dedicated base for alpine sports and recreation on the mountain.

Over the years, the club built four chalets to provide members with authentic alpine accomodation and direct ski-in/ski-out access to the ski fields. However, in 1991, a severe historic storm destroyed the fourth chalet, known as Kea.

Thid event led to extensive renovations and improvements to the remaining chalets to help them withstand extreme mountain weather. The club's history refelcts its resilience and the importance of preserving ints cooperative community spirit for future generations.
