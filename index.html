Welcome to Maunga Club

Enjoy a mountain paradise with Maunga Club! We offer a range of snow and ski chalets excluseively for our Club members.

Ski straight from your chalet. Enjoy 550 hectares of skiing and snowboarding area, with the lonhest winter sports season in New Zealand.

Booking Requirements

  • Only registered Club members can access the chalets.
  • All booking must be from Friday to Sunday.
  • Shared accommodation is available for groups of 6 to 40 people per chalet.

How to Join

Email the following information to our club:

  • Your full name
  • Your physical address
  • Proof of address, such as a copy of a power bill
  • Proof of ID, such as a driver's license or passport

Email: maungaclub@maungaclub.maungaclub

Membership will be confirmed by email. Your membership letter and additional information will be sent by regular mail.
